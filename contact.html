Contact & Support

Effective: 22 August 2026

Need help with your Shaadi Budget Control System purchase or download?

Purchase and download support

Please use the seller/support contact method available through your Payhip purchase confirmation, receipt or download page. This keeps the request connected to the transaction and helps verify the order.

What to include

  • The email address used for the purchase.
  • Your Payhip order reference, if shown.
  • A short description of the issue.
  • A screenshot of the error if it helps explain a technical problem.

Keep sensitive information private

Never send your full card number, CVV, banking password, UPI PIN, Aadhaar OTP or any other authentication code in a support request.

Product scope

Support covers access to the purchased files and reasonable questions about how to start using the workbook. It does not include personal wedding planning, vendor negotiation services, legal advice, tax advice or custom spreadsheet development.
